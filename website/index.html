About Canonify Document Repository

Canonify hosts a centralized, immutable catalog of 206 official medical admission documents covering MBBS, BDS, and allied undergraduate courses. Each publication is cryptographic SHA-256 verified against original government releases, providing transparency, rapid search, and permanent open access for students, researchers, and admissions analysts.
